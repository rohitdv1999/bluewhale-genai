Data Engineer - Lumen Grid Energy

About the role
Lumen Grid collects meter readings from millions of homes. You will build the pipelines that clean and publish this data.

Responsibilities
- Build streaming pipelines with Kafka and Kinesis.
- Model data in PostgreSQL and DynamoDB.
- Orchestrate batch jobs and data quality checks.

Requirements
- 2+ years of experience with Python and SQL.
- Experience with AWS and Docker.

Benefits
Austin, TX.
