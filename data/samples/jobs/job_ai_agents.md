AI Engineer, Agentic Workflows - Brightpath Insurance

About the role
Brightpath is automating claims intake with AI agents that read documents, call internal systems and ask a human adjuster before any payout decision.

Responsibilities
- Build agentic workflows with LangGraph: routing, state management, retries and human approval steps.
- Integrate enterprise APIs and document repositories while respecting permissions.
- Expose internal tools to agents through MCP.
- Write unit, integration and regression tests for agent behaviour.

Requirements
- 4+ years of experience with Python.
- Experience with LLMs, tool calling, structured outputs and Azure OpenAI.
- Experience with RAG and vector search.

Nice to have
- Streamlit prototypes and React front ends.

Benefits
